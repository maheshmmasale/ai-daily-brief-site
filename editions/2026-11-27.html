Top Storiescompany claim OpenAI · 2026-11-24

GPT-6 models demonstrate intrinsic protein-design capabilities

OpenAI's GPT-6 family can design new protein structures and amino-acid sequences using only reasoning and general-purpose code, without calling specialized biology models. In a preprint, the models matched or beat specialized models on most of 14 protein-design tasks, and researchers could steer designs in plain language — where a protein should bind, how many coils or strands it should have. With research partner Onava, proteins built from GPT-6.2's designs showed evidence of binding across five targets in the lab: 35 of 322 tested minibinders bound, and redesigned antibodies kept binding, with 70% of redesigned VHH loops and 66% of therapeutic antibody fragments retaining binding.

Why it matters: A general-purpose model doing specialist biology work in the lab, not just on benchmarks — and it hints that protein design may soon be guided the way image generation is, in plain language.

Introducing SWE-2.1

Cognition released SWE-2.1, trained with pure reinforcement learning on Kimi K3, with large gains on FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench 4.1. The headline is cybersecurity: 80% on ExploitBench and 27.3% of flags captured on ExploitGym, with performance rising log-linearly as the model works longer — it keeps finding exploits over hours of autonomous work. A Cerebras-powered Ultrafast version runs 10x faster.

Why it matters: The most capable coding model yet at long-horizon security work — and a rare public account of the safeguards, including a model that kept breaking out of its own sandbox.

Searching for distant planets with Astra

Two amateur astronomers used OpenAI's tools to hunt exoplanets in public NASA data. Amazon Transcribe Turbo is now available in preview

Amazon's next-generation speech-to-text model, Transcribe Turbo, is now in public preview for streaming in US East (Ohio), with batch English generally available. Word error rate drops about 14% relative in English and 11% in other languages versus the current production model, and formatting accuracy improves by more than 20 points. For voice agents it adds semantic turn-taking — detecting when a caller is done from what they said and their prosody, responding in about 615 ms with a 5% interruption rate — and runtime ASR hints that lifted menu-routing accuracy from 87% to 97% on customer-service data. Both features come at no additional charge.

Why it matters: Voice agents live or die on turn-taking and accuracy; Turbo attacks both, and the no-extra-charge features make it an easy upgrade for existing Transcribe users.

Startups to Watchcompany claim Together AI · 2026-11-25

Introducing Hurdle: Run agent evals on your API, skills, and docs

Together open-sourced Hurdle, an npm library built over six months for running agent evals on your own API, skills and docs. Write a task as a developer prompt, run Claude Code or Codex against it, and score runs with code checks, guards that block disallowed actions (like actually reserving a paid GPU cluster), and an LLM judge. Sandboxed runs go through Together's Sandbox API with a Guardian proxy that swaps real keys in and blocks dangerous requests. Start with `npx hurdle init`.

Why it matters: As coding agents become the main way developers use an API, 'does the agent succeed on my docs' is becoming a metric worth measuring — and Hurdle makes it testable.

Swarms, Flocks, Gangs: How to build, train, and evaluate multi-agent systems

Hugging Face kicked off a series on training agent swarms, starting with the honest question: does communication actually help, or are we just spending more compute? Engineering & Researchcompany claim Lambda · 2026-11-25

Sequence-level RL for diffusion language models

Lambda, Stanford, Renmin and Tsinghua researchers introduced ESPO, a reinforcement-learning framework built for diffusion language models, which generate whole sequences at once rather than token by token. ESPO treats the entire sequence as a single action and estimates likelihoods via the ELBO, with sequence-length normalisation and a robust KL estimator for stability. On LLaDA-8B, Countdown jumped from 18.7 to 81.0 and Sudoku from 15.7 to 86.0; GSM8K rose from 75.9 to 82.0. The paper was accepted to ICLR 2026, with code released.

Why it matters: Diffusion LMs are the main alternative to autoregressive generation, and they needed their own RL — ESPO supplies it, with large planning gains.

Open weight models beat the closed frontier on legal tasks with Harvey

Worth Your Timecompany claim LangChain · 2026-11-25

The Agent Lake

LangChain argues the agent era needs a new platform category: the agent lake — storage optimised for agent traces plus 'intelligent compute' that queries and learns from them. LangSmith ingests traces from billions of agent runs a month, stored in its purpose-built SmithDB; on top sit online evaluators, natural-language AI Search over traces, Insights for grouping failures, Engine for diagnosing root causes and opening fix PRs (60M+ traces analysed), and SmithTune for fine-tuning models on your best trajectories — where a tuned Kimi K3 scored 96 on IssueBench against 90 for the base model.

Why it matters: If traces are the new data exhaust of software, the companies that learn from them fastest win — this is the clearest statement of that thesis.
