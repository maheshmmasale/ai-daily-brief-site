Engineering & Researchcompany claim Hugging Face · 2026-11-03

StateBench: testing how language models track state changes over long documents

The Allen Institute for AI has released StateBench, a four-level benchmark, dataset and code for testing whether language models track changing variables across long documents. The evaluation spans 8 to 64,000 assignments, and all four frontier models tested — Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna — broke down on harder state-tracking forms as inputs grew longer, falling to 13% accuracy or lower on the longest general finite cases.

Why it matters: Long context is not the same as long memory — state tracking is where today's models still quietly fall apart.

OpenAI has introduced LifeVal, a benchmark measuring how well AI helps people complete everyday tasks — from finding childcare to managing finances and government paperwork. Task sources include time-use surveys and a representative survey of 3,995 people about what they most want done on their behalf, with about 100 experienced writers authoring tasks and grading criteria.
